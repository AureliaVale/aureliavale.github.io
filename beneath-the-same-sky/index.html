About this book

There is a question I have carried since I was a teenager. Not a question anyone asked me. One I asked myself, quietly, in the years I was learning English — the language that would shape my entire life as a teacher, and now, much later, as a writer.

The question was this: if I am ever asked, one day, why I did not share what I know — what will I say?

This small book is my answer to forty years of waiting. It is not a theological treatise. It is not a debate. It is not written to convert anyone or to argue with anyone. It is written because every human being on this planet has the right to know — simply to know.

I offer these pages the way I would offer a cup of tea to a curious stranger — warmly, without pressure, with nothing to prove. We live beneath the same sky, you and I.

— Aurelia Vale, from the Preface
What this book is

A quiet, honest introduction to Islam — written in plain English, with warmth and care, for anyone who has ever wondered about this faith but never found the right place to begin.

Not a textbook. Not a polemic. A conversation between two people who live beneath the same sky — one who has lived inside Islam all her life, and one who is curious about what that life feels like from the inside.

All Quranic references are sourced by surah and ayah. All hadith are verified sahih or hasan, sourced to Bukhari, Muslim, or other authenticated collections. Nothing doubtful is included.

About the Author

Aurelia Vale is a retired English teacher and independent author with thirty years of classroom experience in Algeria. She writes literary fiction and literary nonfiction — with care, depth, and the quiet conviction that words, when chosen well, can change how a person sees the world.
