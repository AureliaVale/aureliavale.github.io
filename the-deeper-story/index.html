About this book

Most writing prompts are designed for beginners. They ask you to imagine a scenario, introduce a character, describe a setting. They are useful — the way training wheels are useful. But at some point, the serious writer needs something harder.

The Deeper Story was written for that moment. For the writer who has outgrown the surface — who wants to write fiction that means something, that stays with readers, that asks the questions serious literature has always asked.

There is a difference between a prompt that starts a story and one that starts a real one. This book was built for the second kind.

— Aurelia Vale
What this book contains

Fifty prompts organised across five territories of literary fiction — the areas where serious writing either succeeds or fails. Each prompt is designed not just to generate a scene, but to push the writer toward the deeper questions: what does this character want, what does that wanting cost, what are they unable to say, and what happens when the truth insists on being known.

The book also includes a Story Audit — ten questions to ask any manuscript when something is wrong but you cannot name it — and a Reading List of ten novels chosen specifically to teach the writer who reads them.

About the Author

Aurelia Vale is a retired English teacher and independent author with thirty years of classroom experience in Algeria. She writes literary fiction and literary nonfiction — with care, depth, and the quiet conviction that words, when chosen well, can change how a person sees the world.
