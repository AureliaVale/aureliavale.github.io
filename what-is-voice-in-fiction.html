What Is Voice in Fiction — And How Do You Find Yours?

Voice is the quality in fiction that makes you recognise a writer without seeing their name. It is not style, exactly, though it includes style. It is not tone, though tone is part of it. It is something closer to a sensibility — a particular way of noticing, valuing, and rendering the world.

Voice cannot be manufactured. It can only be discovered — and the discovery comes through writing. A great deal of writing. Writing that is honest rather than impressive, specific rather than general, willing to be strange in the ways that you, specifically, are strange.

The writers who have found their voice are often the ones who stopped trying to write like someone else. The influence is still there — all writers are shaped by what they have read — but it has been digested rather than imitated. It has become part of something new.

Write the thing only you could write. That is where your voice lives.

The Deeper Story was written for writers who want to work at this depth.
